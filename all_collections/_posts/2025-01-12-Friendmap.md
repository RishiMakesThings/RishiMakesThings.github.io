Do you have friends??????? Are linkedin connections getting you down??? Do you want to map out all your friends systematically
so you can enjoy the fruits of your social prowess?? [Well do I have the site for you!](../../friendmap)

I made this after I started to make a little friendweb on paper, thought to myself "wow, would sure love it I could just type all the names out in a table and just watch the names arrange themselves into a web of connections" and then realised that I actually have free will and nothing going on this week.

_**Disclaimer**: Use of the friend map requires you to have friends. If you do not have friends go outside and make some friends and then return to this website to construct your very own friendmap as I (unfortunately) cannot supply them._
